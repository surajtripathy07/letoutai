Questions

Is Sally a person or an AI?

Sally is an AI agent. She says so when a tenant asks, and a person from the flat meets the tenant at the visit.

What if Sally does not know the answer?

She asks the operator on WhatsApp and replies to the tenant after the operator answers.

Which areas does Sally cover?

Mumbai. Beds have filled in Powai and Marol. Tenants message one number and Sally knows every bed on the network.

How is this different from a broker?

A broker charges a month’s rent. Sally charges Rs 3,200 on the signed agreement, and nothing until a bed fills.

How long does onboarding take?

A day. The operator shares flats and house rules, connects a WhatsApp number and approves the first messages on cards.

What does Sally cost a tenant?

Nothing. LetoutAI earns from the operator.
